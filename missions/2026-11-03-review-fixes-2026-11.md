## Design Notes

- C1: legal ATT write payload max = setMTU(517)−3 = 514, so RXQ_MAX=514 makes legitimate writes pass intact; anything larger is dropped whole (+counter, first-time Serial warn) instead of truncated-then-masked (silent corruption).
- C2/C3: boot-time OOM becomes diagnosable fail-fast (log name/size/free/largest-block → ESP.restart) instead of NULL+offset wild writes.
- W1a: keep existing 3s→saveAndRestart self-heal; only the final portMAX_DELAY fallback becomes 2s→degrade to library blit path (fastDev=nullptr).
- W1b: subscribe loopTask to TWDT (5s/panic already in sdkconfig) after verifying no >5s blocking segment; unsubscribe before Shutdown's NimBLE stop if needed.
- W2: take flushDone (100ms timeout) before mutating rotation globals, mirroring flushSync take/give convention.
- W4: 4×char[48] FIFO for pendingAck, drain-all at notify site; overflow drops oldest + counts.
